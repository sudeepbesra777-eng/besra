## LICENSING — read once, saves you a strike later

| Source | Licence reality | The trap |
|---|---|---|
| **NASA** | Most media is public domain and free to use commercially | **Not all of it.** NASA hosts partner imagery (ESA, JAXA, contractors) that is *not* public domain. Check the individual asset page every time. Also: never use NASA's logo/insignia, and never imply NASA endorses your channel. |
| **NOAA / USGS / NSF** | US Government works, generally public domain | Same partner-content caveat |
| **Pexels / Pixabay** | Free for commercial use, no attribution required | Can't resell the unaltered file; care with identifiable people or trademarks in frame |
| **Videvo / Coverr** | Mixed — some free, some require attribution | **Check the per-clip licence**, it varies within the same site |
| **Freesound** | Per-sound licences differ | **CC-BY needs credit. Non-commercial licences are unusable on a monetised channel.** Filter to CC0 to stay safe. |
| **YouTube Audio Library** | Cleared for YouTube | Some tracks require attribution — the library tells you which |
| **AI generators** | **Free tiers often forbid commercial use** | Check the specific tool's terms *before* generating. A free tier that bans commercial use is useless for a monetised channel — this is the single most-missed licensing issue in faceless video. |

**One editorial rule that isn't legal but matters more:** this is a real expedition. Never
present AI-generated imagery as actual footage of it. The stylised look does that work for
you — keep it stylised, and consider a line in your description saying visuals are
illustrative reconstructions.
