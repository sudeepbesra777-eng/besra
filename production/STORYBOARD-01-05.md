# Storyboard — Blocks 1 to 5

Audio stays on the 10-second grid (block N starts at (N-1)x10s), which gives ~2s of air after
each line. Visuals are free to change inside and across those blocks.

## STYLE LOCK — paste at the end of EVERY image prompt, unchanged

```
Cinematic documentary illustration, stylized 3D scientific visualization, deliberately not
photorealistic archival footage. Cold desaturated palette: bone white, glacial teal, deep navy
black. Volumetric light, soft atmospheric haze, fine film grain. Wide cinematic 16:9
composition, shallow depth of field. No people, no faces, no text, no letters, no numbers,
no logos, no watermarks.
```

Two rules that come with it:
- **Never generate text.** All titles and labels go on in Shotcut on track V2, where they will
  be sharp, correct and consistent.
- **Non-photoreal on purpose.** This is a real expedition. Photoreal AI output would read as
  fabricated documentary footage, which is both dishonest and fatal to a science channel's
  credibility. Stylized reads as deliberate art direction.

## BLOCK 01 - 0:00 to 0:10 - TWO visuals

> "There's a hole in Antarctica. Two feet wide. It drops six hundred metres into water no light has touched."

**Communicating:** an impossible-sounding object exists. Establish scale and dread in ten seconds.
**Why two visuals:** the line has two beats - the place, then the hole itself. One image cannot
carry both, and cutting on "two feet wide" lands the scale.

### 01A - `img01a.png` - 0:00 to 0:05
- **Type:** AI image
- **Concept:** vast empty white plain to a flat horizon; a single small dark dot near centre. The
  emptiness is the point - the viewer should not immediately know what they are looking at.
- **Camera in Shotcut:** slow push-in, zoom 100% to 108%
- **On-screen text:** none. Let the image sit.
- **Prompt:**
```
A vast empty Antarctic ice plain stretching to a flat horizon under a low overcast sky, seen
from a high aerial angle. Fine dry snow streams in low ribbons across the surface. A single
small dark circular opening sits near the centre of the frame, tiny against the emptiness.
Flat shadowless polar daylight, no visible sun.
```
+ STYLE LOCK

### 01B - `img01b.png` - 0:05 to 0:10
- **Type:** AI image
- **Concept:** the same hole, now close. A clean circular shaft cut through pale blue ice,
  interior falling away to pure black. No bottom visible - that is the whole idea.
- **Camera in Shotcut:** slow push-in, zoom 100% to 112%, slight downward drift
- **On-screen text:** none
- **Prompt:**
```
Close overhead view of a narrow circular borehole cut cleanly through thick pale blue glacial
ice, roughly half a metre wide. The shaft walls are smooth and translucent near the surface and
fall away into absolute blackness. Wind-blown snow grains scatter across the ice around the rim.
Flat overcast polar light above, no light reaching inside the shaft.
```

## BLOCK 02 - 0:10 to 0:20 - ONE visual

> "In 2020, scientists lowered a robot down it. They found they had been measuring the wrong thing entirely."

**Communicating:** human intent enters the story, then immediately gets overturned.
**Concept:** a slender yellow robot suspended on a cable inside the shaft, its lamp throwing a
small pool of light on the ice walls. The yellow is the first warm colour in the film - that
contrast is deliberate and worth protecting in the grade.

- **Type:** AI image
- **File:** `img02.png`
- **Duration:** 10s
- **Camera in Shotcut:** slow downward pan (position drifts up so the frame appears to descend),
  plus a gentle zoom 100% to 106%. The descent is the whole feeling of this shot.
- **On-screen text:** none
- **Prompt:**
```
A slender yellow torpedo-shaped underwater robot suspended on a thin cable inside a narrow
vertical shaft of pale blue ice, seen from slightly above and behind. A single lamp on its nose
throws a small pool of light onto the translucent ice walls. Below it the shaft falls away into
darkness. Cold blue ice, one warm yellow accent.
```
+ STYLE LOCK

## BLOCK 04 - 0:30 to 0:40 - ONE visual

> "Antarctica isn't land with snow on top. It's rock, buried under an ice sheet four kilometres thick."

**Communicating:** correcting a wrong mental model. The viewer must SEE the rock underneath.
**Concept:** a cross-section cut through the continent, presented as a physical museum model
rather than a screen graphic - matte surfaces, real light. Dark bedrock at the base, an enormous
pale ice mass above it, ocean to one side. Horizontal, layered, calm.

- **Type:** AI image
- **File:** `img04.png`
- **Duration:** 10s
- **Camera in Shotcut:** slow lateral pan left to right, no zoom. The sideways move reads as
  "surveying" and contrasts with the push-ins before it.
- **On-screen text (V2):** small label `4 km` with a thin vertical measure line over the ice mass,
  appearing at ~0:36. Built in Shotcut, not generated.
- **Prompt:**
```
A clean cross-section diorama of a polar ice sheet viewed directly from the side against a dark
empty background. Dark grey bedrock forms the base, an enormous pale blue-white ice mass sits
above it filling most of the frame, and dark ocean water meets the ice at the right edge. Matte
self-illuminated surfaces like a physical museum model under even studio light. Layers clearly
separated.
```
+ STYLE LOCK

## BLOCK 05 - 0:40 to 0:50 - ONE visual

> "Stand on that surface, and the real ground sits further below you than a jet flies above you."

**Communicating:** making four kilometres felt rather than known.
**Concept:** a tall vertical composition inside a 16:9 frame - bedrock at the very bottom, a
column of ice, the surface line, empty sky above, and a tiny aircraft silhouette high up. The
verticality is the argument, and it deliberately contrasts with block 04's horizontal layers so
two diagram-style shots in a row don't feel repetitive.

- **Type:** AI image
- **File:** `img05.png`
- **Duration:** 10s
- **Camera in Shotcut:** slow vertical pan **downward** (start framed high near the aircraft, end
  low at the bedrock). Zoom stays at 100%. The move performs the sentence.
- **On-screen text (V2):** none yet - or optionally a thin measure line. Keep it clean; the
  composition already carries the idea.
- **Prompt:**
```
A tall narrow vertical cross-section seen from the side against a dark background: dark bedrock
at the very bottom, above it a towering column of pale blue glacial ice, a flat bright snow
surface line, then vast empty pale sky filling the upper half with a tiny distant aircraft
silhouette near the top. Extreme sense of vertical scale, thin atmospheric haze between layers.
```
+ STYLE LOCK
